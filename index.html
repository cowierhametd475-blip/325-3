About UP QIB

UK-based professional financial information consulting firm focusing on global cross-border financial data and investment advisory

Our Company Profile

UP QIB is a registered financial information consultancy enterprise located in Manchester, England. We were founded to break information barriers for global investors and provide transparent, objective and data-backed financial consulting services.

Our core team consists of senior financial analysts, data scientists and cross-border investment experts with decades of experience in international capital markets. We serve retail investors, asset management firms, listed companies and financial service agencies across Europe, Asia and North America.

Our Core Values

  • Data Objectivity: All analysis conclusions based on verified real market data
  • Client Priority: Customized consulting solutions for every client’s financial goals
  • Global Vision: Cover multi-region financial markets without regional bias
  • Risk Prudence: Emphasize risk disclosure in all investment advice outputs

Our Development Vision

We aim to build a world-class integrated financial information service platform, unify global multi-market data resources, lower the threshold of professional financial analysis, and become the most trusted consulting partner for global capital participants.

We continuously upgrade our data technology system, expand analyst team scale, and launch diversified data subscription and one-on-one consulting packages to meet the growing demand of complex global financial transactions.
